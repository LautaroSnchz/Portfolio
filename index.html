About me

I am a Full Stack Developer with a focus on advanced frontend: I build scalable and self-managed applications and pages using React, paying close attention to performance, architecture, and intuitive user experiences. Since 2022, I have been gaining experience through personal and collaborative projects, applying best practices in architecture and debugging. My tech stack is built on React, Vite, and TypeScript for the frontend, and Node.js + MongoDB for APIs and services. On the backend, I also work with modern C#/.NET and SQL Server. I manage the full development lifecycle, from coding to deployment: I take care of hosting, production releases, and continuous updates for each project, while deepening my knowledge of DevOps practices. My goal is to keep growing, delivering solid and scalable solutions in collaborative environments.
